## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an unapologetic, cinematic, and aggressively disciplined digital identity tailored for CampusOps. Merging brutalist editorial rigor with futuristic athletic precision, the aesthetic leverages heavy contrast, monumental typography, and razor-sharp accents to command attention.

### Personality & Demographics
- **Tone:** Authoritative, kinetic, razor-sharp, uncompromising, and industrial.
- **Audience:** Campus administrators, operations directors, athletic leads, and high-performance teams requiring operational clarity at speed.
- **Emotional Response:** Inspires decisive action, confidence, momentum, and operational dominance without unnecessary fluff.

### Design Movement: Cinematic High-Contrast Editorial
The visual signature combines deep obsidian blackness (65% dominant volume), chalky cream structural planes (25% grounding volume), and hyper-luminescent lime energy vectors (10% targeted accent volume). Layouts juxtapose condensed typographic scale with strict structural grids, framed by dark charcoal containers and ethereal, razor-thin glowing lime orbital arcs.

## Colors

The system enforces a dogmatic tri-tone hierarchy. Under no circumstances may blue, purple, red, orange, or multi-hue gradients enter the interface.

### Strict Ratio Distribution
1. **Pitch Black (`#0A0A0A` - 65%):** Canvas foundation, full-bleed backdrops, and absolute structural void.
2. **Cream (`#F2EFE9` - 25%):** Editorial contrast sections, inverted narrative panels, primary body copy on dark surfaces, and secondary badges.
3. **Hyper Lime (`#C5F135` - 10%):** Interactive affordances, critical metrics, glowing atmospheric contours, and focused typographic hits.
4. **Structural Charcoal (`#141414` & `#1C1C1C`):** Surface containers, recessed panels, card fills, and low-contrast borders.

### Strict Application Rules
- **Buttons:** Lime `#C5F135` filled surfaces must exclusively use `#0A0A0A` text and icons.
- **Text On Black (`#0A0A0A` / `#141414`):** Primary text rendered in Cream `#F2EFE9`; highlighted phrases and accent tokens rendered strictly in Lime `#C5F135`.
- **Text On Cream (`#F2EFE9`):** Primary text rendered strictly in Black `#0A0A0A`; highlights and micro badges rendered in Lime `#C5F135` (paired with dark outlines) or solid black.
- **Lime Typography:** Never display Lime text against Cream surfaces; Lime text is strictly reserved for `#0A0A0A` and `#141414` environments to preserve legibility and punch.

## Typography

The typographical engine matches imposing condensed uppercase display titles against cold, rational sans-serif body prose.

### Display & Headlines (Oswald)
- Always set in full uppercase (`text-transform: uppercase`) for hero, headline, and section titles.
- Tightly tracked with a line height ratio approaching 1:1, mimicking editorial billboards and high-fashion sports journalism.
- Two-tone titular split: pair white/cream words alongside selective lime highlight words within the exact same heading block.

### Body & Microcopy (Inter)
- Delivers crisp legibility and technical neutrality.
- Formatted in natural sentence case at relaxed line-height ratios (1.5x - 1.6x) to balance the density of the condensed headlines.
- Technical metadata, operational metrics, and kicker subheadings use `label-caps` in uppercase with aggressive letter tracking.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a rigid 12-column architectural grid punctuated by asymmetrical split-screen panels and massive full-bleed hero frames.

### Grid Rhythm & Adapting Viewports
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, 24px gutters, 40px outer safe margin. Generous vertical section rhythm (80px to 120px) creating breathing space around heavy typography.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, 20px gutters, 32px outer safe margin. Multi-column editorial cards condense into 2x2 modular patterns.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, 12px gutters, 20px outer margin. Asymmetrical cards stack into a unified vertical feed; display typography downscales via mobile tokens to retain punch without wrapping single words into micro fragments.

### Compositional Rules
- Use intentional section alternation: transition directly from high-density black charcoal panels (`#0A0A0A`) into full-width cream panels (`#F2EFE9`) to deliver visceral rhythm during scroll depth.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through tonal layering, sharp outline delineation, and ethereal luminous glows rather than traditional diffuse drop shadows.

### Tonal Hierarchy
1. **Base Layer (`#0A0A0A`):** The master canvas.
2. **Mid Layer (`#141414`):** Cards, panels, sidebars, and structural drawers.
3. **Top Layer (`#1C1C1C`):** Hover states, nested metric cards, dropdown menus, and popovers.

### Borders & Glowing Accents
- **Hairline Outlines:** Dark containers employ a crisp 1px border colored `#1C1C1C` or `#262626`.
- **Glowing Lime Curved Lines:** Thin (1px to 1.5px) vector rings and orbital arcs tinted in `#C5F135` cut across cards and photography corners. They carry a localized blur glow:
  - Outer glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px -2px rgba(197, 241, 53, 0.4)`.
  - Inner ray: `drop-shadow(0 0 8px rgba(197, 241, 53, 0.7))`.
- Pure floating overlays on dark surfaces use deep black ambient occlusion (`box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`).

## Shapes

The design uses a controlled roundedness scale (`level 2`) to balance raw brutalist density with slick, high-tech modern hardware aesthetics.

### Boundary Radius Matrix
- **Cards, Modals & Structural Panels:** 16px (`rounded-lg` / `1rem`) corner radius.
- **Standard Controls & Text Inputs:** 8px (`0.5rem`) corner radius.
- **Interactive Action Pills, Badges & Icon Nodes:** Full radius (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to create organic tension against rectangular modules.
- **Architectural Motif:** Recurring use of smooth circular rings and sweeping vector quarter-arcs framing hero elements.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Lime `#C5F135` background, solid Black `#0A0A0A` text, weight 700. In button clusters, accompanied by an integrated circular arrow badge (`→`) on the trailing edge. Hover: scales up to `1.02x` with an outer glow `0 0 24px rgba(197, 241, 53, 0.5)`.
- **Secondary Action (Dark Canvas):** Surface `#141414`, border 1px solid `#1C1C1C`, text `#F2EFE9`. Hover: border transitions to `#C5F135`, text transitions to `#C5F135`.
- **Secondary Action (Cream Canvas):** Solid Black `#0A0A0A` background, Cream `#F2EFE9` text. Hover: opacity transitions to 90%.

### Cards & Panels
- **Dark Metric Cards:** `#141414` fill, 1px `#1C1C1C` border, 16px corner radius. Padding strictly `space-lg` (24px). Headers set in Oswald uppercase, body metrics set in huge Oswald display typography, auxiliary stats in Lime `#C5F135`.
- **Editorial Contrast Cards:** Pure Cream `#F2EFE9` background with deep Black `#0A0A0A` text. Micro-badges on cream cards feature solid black background with crisp cream typography.

### Input Fields
- Fill: `#141414`, outline: 1px `#1C1C1C`, text: `#F2EFE9`, placeholder: `#525252`.
- Focus State: 1px solid `#C5F135` with subtle lime ambient glow (`0 0 10px rgba(197, 241, 53, 0.25)`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Unchecked: `#141414` interior with 1px `#262626` border.
- Checked: Solid `#C5F135` fill with Black `#0A0A0A` checkmark/bullet indicator.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding 4px 12px.
- Dark Badge: Background `#1C1C1C`, border 1px solid rgba(197, 241, 53, 0.3), text `#C5F135`, typography `label-caps`.
- Light Badge: Background `#0A0A0A`, text `#F2EFE9`, typography `label-caps`.

### Atmospheric Vector Rings
- SVG-based circular tracks with gradient transparency fades terminating in pure `#C5F135` stroke, creating a futuristic lens effect over cards and masked imagery.
